Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Dope Lab ("we", "our", "us") builds apps that respect your privacy. This policy explains how our apps handle your data.

NowPlaying Relay

What the app does: NowPlaying Relay is an Apple Music player that can relay information about the currently playing track to webhook endpoints you configure.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Dope Lab does not operate servers that receive, store, or process your information. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No crash reporting sent to us
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No advertising or ad identifiers

Data That Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to Dope Lab:

  • Apple Music library access: Used to browse and play your music. This data stays within the app and Apple's MusicKit framework.
  • Webhook configuration: Endpoint URLs and authentication tokens you configure are stored in your device's secure keychain.
  • Activity logs: Webhook delivery logs are stored locally for your reference.

Data You Choose to Share

If you enable the webhook relay feature, the app sends currently-playing track metadata (song title, artist, album, artwork URL) to webhook endpoints you configure. This data is sent directly from your device to your chosen endpoints. Dope Lab does not receive, intercept, or have access to this data.

You are in full control of:

  • Whether the relay is enabled or disabled
  • Which endpoints receive data
  • What events trigger a relay

Third-Party Services

NowPlaying Relay uses Apple's MusicKit framework to access Apple Music. Your use of Apple Music is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@dopelab.com.
